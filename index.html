FAQ

Questions, answered.

Short, useful answers to the questions a visitor is likely to have before reaching out.

Am I a candidate for hair restoration?+

Every patient is different. A consultation helps determine whether surgical or non-surgical options may be appropriate for you.

What's the difference between FUE and DHI?+

Both involve transplanted hair follicles, but the techniques differ in how grafts are prepared and implanted.

How long does a procedure take?+

Timing depends on the treatment plan, number of grafts, and individual circumstances.

When will I see results?+

Hair growth takes time and varies between individuals. A clinician can explain the expected timeline for your situation.

Is hair transplantation painful?+

Clinics generally use local anesthesia during surgical procedures, but individual experiences vary.

How do I start?+

Request a consultation so the clinical team can discuss your goals and possible treatment options.
