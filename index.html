Questions

Before you book.

The FAQ is here to remove uncertainty, not to fill empty space at the bottom of a template.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?+

A consultation and assessment should come before a treatment recommendation. Hair-loss pattern, donor characteristics, goals and expectations all influence the discussion.

How many grafts might I need?+

There is no universal number. A clinician should assess the area requiring coverage, existing density, donor supply and the desired result before estimating graft requirements.

When can I expect to see changes?+

Hair restoration is a gradual process. A real clinic should explain expected milestones and the possibility of temporary shedding or uneven growth during recovery.

What should I look for in before-and-after photos?+

Look for consistent lighting and angles, a visible donor area, clear timelines and enough cases to judge the clinic's work across different patterns of hair loss.

Can I get an online consultation?+

Many clinics can conduct an initial remote assessment. The final site can include a secure photo-upload workflow and a video consultation option where appropriate.
